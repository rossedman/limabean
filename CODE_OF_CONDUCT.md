# Code of conduct

limabean follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive with everyone in issues, pull
requests and discussions; harassment and personal attacks aren't welcome.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer,
[@rossedman](https://github.com/rossedman). Reports are handled privately,
and the maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions, or block
people, who don't follow it.
